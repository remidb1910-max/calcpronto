Method and sources

Every CalcPronto calculator applies official, dated rules. Before every release, 459 automated tests check the 12 reference calculation engines, then 493,853 checks compare the website with those engines. Last full verification: November 27, 2026.

How each calculator is checked

  1. Rules taken from official texts. Every rate, ceiling and scale comes from a dated official source: Légifrance, BOFiP, impots.gouv.fr, Urssaf, Service-Public.fr, the Official Journal of the European Union… The sources of each calculator are cited at the bottom of its page and listed below. When a rule is uncertain, the calculator leaves it out of scope and says so, rather than guessing.
  2. A reference engine, tested on official examples. Each calculation is first written as a reference engine (in Python), with automated tests that reproduce the worked examples published by the authorities: the French notaries' fee scale, income tax examples, the transfer tax rate of each French département, and so on. The same engines answer AI assistants through the CalcPronto connector.
  3. The website compared with the engine, to the cent. The calculators on this site run in your browser. For each one, hundreds to thousands of cases are computed by the reference engine, then computed again by the website in a simulated browser: every amount is compared to the cent, along with the titles, tables, links and pre-filled forms of the pages by amount.
  4. Nothing is published if a check fails. The website and the connector are only released when every test and every check passes. The figures on this page are produced automatically from those tests each time the site is built.

Rate updates

Most scales change on 1 January: the French budget act (income tax scale), the social security ceiling and minimum wage, the VAT rates of some countries, the transfer tax rates voted by French départements. Others change during the year (the neutral withholding tax rate, reduced VAT rates).

Every change known for a given date goes into a review calendar. As soon as the new figures are officially published, the engine, its tests and the pages are updated and checked again; until then, the calculator keeps applying the rules in force. Where a calculator depends on yearly scales, the year applied is shown on it.

Every week, calculators drawn at random are recomputed from the official sources of the day, independently of their code; any difference is fixed at once.
