Privacy policy

Calculations run in your browser: the amounts, dates and trips you enter are not sent to our servers and are not stored.

The site sets no analytics cookies. The host (GitHub Pages) may log your IP address for technical and security reasons.

If ads are shown, they are served by Google AdSense. Your consent is requested before any advertising cookie through a certified consent management platform, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Some pages contain partner links, marked "Advertisement". If you buy a service after following one of them, the publisher may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The partner may set a tracking cookie on its own site. These links change neither the calculations nor their results.

For the CalcPronto connector in AI assistants, we keep your email address (Google sign-in), your credit balance and your purchase history. Payments are processed by our reseller, Polar. This data is used only to run the service.

Under the GDPR you can access, correct or delete your data by writing to the contact address in the legal notice. You can also complain to the French data protection authority (CNIL).
